Common questions

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You simply forward calls to us, and you can turn forwarding off any time.

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and answers in your company's name. If a caller asks, it says honestly that it's an automated assistant.

What if someone wants a real person?

It transfers the call or takes a message, based on the rules you set.

Is there a contract?

Yes — a simple services agreement that protects both of us. Founding clients commit to an initial 3-month term; after that it's month to month.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how your calls are handled today. We'll give you a go-live date on our first call, and you approve everything before it starts answering.

Do you handle calls in Spanish?

Yes — the receptionist can answer in Spanish as well as English. We're still actively testing this feature, so ask us about current coverage for your service area.
